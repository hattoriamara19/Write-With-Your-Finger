How to use AirWrite

1️⃣ Start

Allow camera permission.

2️⃣ Raise finger

Show your hand clearly to the camera.

3️⃣ Write

Move your index finger to write.

4️⃣ Pause

Lower your finger or close your hand to stop writing.

💡 Tip: Use good lighting and keep your hand inside the camera view.
